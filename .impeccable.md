## Design Context

### Users
Internal admins and operations team members use this dashboard to manage operational data, content, reservations, categories, and business settings quickly and accurately.

### Brand Personality
Clean, enterprise, reliable. The interface should feel professional, organized, and efficient rather than playful or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a polished light admin-dashboard style that fits the existing PrimeNG/Bootstrap system. Favor clear hierarchy, restrained color, crisp tables, readable forms, and predictable actions. Make feature pages visually stronger than the older default CRUD screens while staying consistent with the current application.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize scanning speed for operational workflows.
- Keep controls compact, aligned, and easy to compare.
- Use restrained visual polish: subtle borders, calm surfaces, clear hover/focus states.
- Make action hierarchy obvious without making every control visually loud.
- Preserve existing PrimeNG patterns and improve them where they affect usability.
